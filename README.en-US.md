ChatGPT is a conversational AI chatbot prototype developed by OpenAI. The chatbot is a large language model that employs supervised learning and reinforcement learning techniques. Launched in November 2022, ChatGPT gained attention for its detailed and clear responses, despite criticism regarding the factual accuracy of its answers. ChatGPT was fine-tuned and upgraded on GPT-3.5 using supervised learning and reinforcement learning. Its underlying models were trained in collaboration with Microsoft on Azure's supercomputing infrastructure. ChatGPT's training data includes manual pages, internet phenomena, and knowledge of programming languages, such as Bulletin Board Systems (BBS) and Python.
